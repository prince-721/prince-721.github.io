About

How I work

I like projects that start from a real annoyance — forgetting to drink water, losing track of a watchlist, scrolling reels for hours without realizing it, retyping a song queue by hand — and end as a small tool that just handles it.

That's meant working across a variety of stacks: React Native, Node.js & Express for health systems, Kotlin with Jetpack Compose & MVVM for Android background services, and plain JavaScript for high-performance Chrome extensions. Most recently I've been building with AI in the loop (Groq API, prompt engineering, and context compression) to supercharge developer workflows.

I believe the best software is the kind that quietly works in the background — tracking, organizing, or automating something people already do — then gets out of the way. I'm currently seeking internships and full-time opportunities where I can solve high-impact engineering problems.
